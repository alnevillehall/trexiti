### LinkedIn caption

Your employees should not be the integration layer between WhatsApp, email, spreadsheets, calendars, paper and accounting software.

But in many businesses, that is exactly what happens.

People copy information from one place to another.
They remember which customer needs a follow-up.
They chase another department for status.
They reconstruct the same record every time work changes hands.

The team may look disorganized from the outside.
Often, the real problem is that the systems are fragmented.

Before adding another platform, map five things:

1. Where information enters.
2. Who owns the next action.
3. What must be recorded.
4. Which system should be the source of truth.
5. What should happen automatically after each meaningful event.

The goal is not always to replace everything.

Keep what works.
Connect what should remain.
Build only where the business genuinely needs its own system.

That is where Trexiti starts.

## Day 3 — Thursday, August 13

### 60–90 second founder video script

**Hook — 0:00–0:10**

Most businesses automate too early.

They take a process nobody fully understands, add technology, and make the confusion run faster.

**Body — 0:10–0:55**

Before you automate anything, map five things.

First: what event starts the workflow?
A customer inquiry? A payment? A completed job?

Second: who owns the next action?
Not “the team.” One role or one person.

Third: what information has to move with the work?
Customer details, approval, status, amount, due date—whatever the next decision depends on.

Fourth: where is the source of truth?
If three systems disagree, which one wins?

Fifth: what can fail?
Missing data, no response, rejected payment, unavailable staff. Good automation needs a human path for exceptions.

**Close — 0:55–1:15**

Automation should strengthen a sound process, not hide a broken one.

At Trexiti, we understand the workflow first, then decide what should be simplified, connected, automated or built.

What process in your business causes the most friction right now?
