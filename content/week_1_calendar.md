# Trexiti Launch Week 1 Content Calendar

Timezone: America/Jamaica  
Launch window: Tuesday, August 11–Monday, August 17, 2026

## Publishing rhythm

Use Al's LinkedIn profile as the primary publishing account. The Trexiti Company Page should repost or publish a shorter company version 30–90 minutes later. Instagram receives the visual version of the same idea. Do not create seven unrelated campaigns.

Suggested posting windows:

- LinkedIn founder: 7:15–8:00 a.m.
- Trexiti Company Page: 9:00–10:30 a.m.
- Instagram: 6:30–8:00 p.m.
- Founder response window: 15 minutes after posting, 10 minutes at lunch, 20 minutes in the evening.

These are operating times, not promises of algorithmic superiority. Keep them consistent for the first month, then adjust using actual data.
