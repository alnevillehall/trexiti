# Trexiti Lead Readiness Audit

This note is the practical sales lens for the website. The site should help a prospect understand the operational problem, see a believable example, trust the offer, and know exactly how to start.

## Lead Positioning

Trexiti should be pitched as an AI systems and operational software company, not a web design shop. The strongest message is:

Trexiti builds intelligent systems that help companies replace scattered workflows with software, automation, dashboards, and AI-assisted operating control.

## Best First Targets

1. Real estate operators
Property managers, landlords, developers, and real estate service teams handling tenant requests, maintenance, contractors, owners, inspections, documents, and reporting.

2. Operations-heavy businesses
Companies where work moves through spreadsheets, WhatsApp, calls, approvals, manual follow-ups, disconnected tools, or field teams.

3. Growing owner-led companies
Businesses that have outgrown basic websites and need internal systems, visibility, automation, and repeatable operating workflows.

## Pain Signals To Look For

- Requests arrive through too many channels.
- Managers do not know the real status of work without asking staff.
- Contractors or field teams need better dispatch, updates, and proof of completion.
- Owners, clients, or tenants ask for updates before the team has clear answers.
- Reporting takes too long because data lives in different places.
- The company has a website, but the business itself still runs manually.
- Staff spend time copying data, chasing reminders, or recreating the same reports.

## Website Sales Path

1. Homepage
Use this as the lead's first impression. It explains that Trexiti builds operational systems, not basic websites, and shows a real-world example path.

2. PropertyOS
Use this for real estate leads. It gives prospects a concrete platform example they can understand immediately.

3. Services
Use this for non-real-estate leads. It shows Trexiti can build custom systems, AI automation, dashboards, web apps, mobile apps, integrations, and workflow infrastructure.

4. Contact / Systems Audit
Use this as the conversion page. The offer is low-pressure and diagnostic: Trexiti reviews workflows and shows where software, automation, and AI can improve operations.

## Outreach Angle

Open with the business problem, not the technology.

Example:

Your team may already have a website, but the real operational drag is usually behind the scenes: maintenance requests, customer updates, staff follow-ups, owner reporting, spreadsheets, and manual coordination. Trexiti builds the systems that make that work visible and easier to control.

## Example To Show Prospects

The homepage now includes an example transformation based on a property management company:

- Before: WhatsApp, calls, spreadsheets, manual contractor coordination, and owner updates only when requested.
- Trexiti system: request intake, AI-assisted routing, contractor dispatch, owner visibility, and management dashboards.
- Outcome to emphasize: every request becomes visible from intake to resolution.

## Conversion Checklist

- Clear primary CTA: Book a Systems Audit.
- Real estate proof point: PropertyOS.
- Service breadth: custom software, AI automation, dashboards, apps, cloud integrations, process optimization.
- Buyer clarity: best-fit prospects and qualification signals are visible on the homepage.
- Trust copy: no pressure, no generic pitch, diagnostic first step.
- Differentiation: Trexiti does not just build screens; it builds systems that help businesses run better.
