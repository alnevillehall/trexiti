# Trexiti UI Money Audit

This audit reviews the public website against the buyer profile: realtors, brokerages, property operators, developers, asset owners, and corporate teams with real budgets.

## Original Risk

The UI already looked premium and futuristic, but parts of the message still leaned too much on what Trexiti is:

- AI systems company
- operational software
- automation platforms
- intelligent infrastructure

That is accurate, but higher-value buyers need to quickly see what the technology protects or improves for them:

- portfolio visibility
- owner reporting
- revenue signals
- service quality
- lead flow
- operational control
- time saved by management teams
- workflow reliability at scale

## Buyer-Facing Direction

The site should lead with the client's business reality:

- Realtors and brokerages need better lead follow-up, listing workflows, client updates, deal tasks, and team visibility.
- Property managers need tenant request intake, maintenance control, contractor dispatch, rent status, owner reporting, and portfolio dashboards.
- Developers and asset owners need project visibility, property records, inspection history, documents, capital improvement tracking, and stakeholder reporting.
- Corporate teams need internal systems, approval flows, dashboards, integrations, automation, and executive visibility.

## Changes Made

- Homepage hero now speaks directly to realtors, property companies, developers, and corporate teams.
- Trust strip now highlights buyer outcomes: portfolio operations, brokerage workflows, owner reporting, revenue visibility, and enterprise systems.
- Added a buyer-specific section for brokerages, property managers, developers, asset owners, and corporate operators.
- Added a commercial outcomes section focused on revenue discipline, owner confidence, portfolio control, and scaling without chaos.
- PropertyOS now presents portfolio visibility, revenue discipline, owner-ready reporting, and service quality at scale.
- Services page now connects services to commercial use cases for revenue teams, property operators, executives, and growing companies.
- Industries page now prioritizes brokerages, realtor teams, property management companies, developers, asset owners, and corporate operations.
- Contact page now qualifies serious buyers with portfolio/company scale and investment readiness.

## What The Site Now Sells

Trexiti no longer just sells software. The site now sells control over expensive operational complexity.

The strongest promise is:

Trexiti helps real estate and corporate operators make workflows, revenue signals, owner reporting, service quality, and team execution visible from one intelligent operating layer.

## Next Production Upgrade

The contact form currently opens an email client. To fully support lead capture from paid traffic or outreach, the next upgrade should connect the form to the private admin CRM using a database-backed submission flow.
