# The Business Systems Friction Checklist

## Intro copy

This checklist helps a business identify where customers, employees, information and money are being held together by manual effort rather than a reliable system.

Score every statement:

- **0 — Rarely or never**
- **1 — Sometimes**
- **2 — Frequently**

## Customer and sales

1. Customer enquiries arrive through several channels without one shared record.
2. Follow-up depends on a person remembering or checking old messages.
3. Customers repeat information they have already supplied.
4. Management cannot easily see which enquiries became sales.

## Work and operations

5. Staff repeatedly enter the same information into different tools.
6. Ownership of the next action is sometimes unclear.
7. Job, project or request status is difficult to confirm quickly.
8. Work slows down significantly when one key employee is absent.
9. Exceptions are handled through private messages rather than a visible workflow.

## Information and tools

10. Important information lives across spreadsheets, WhatsApp, email, paper or notes.
11. Different teams maintain different versions of the same record.
12. The business has software that is underused because it does not fit the workflow.
13. Reports require manual reconciliation between systems.
14. It is unclear which system should be the source of truth.

## Finance and management

15. Completed work does not always lead to an invoice promptly.
16. Outstanding balances are difficult to see in one place.
17. Management reporting takes too long to assemble.
18. Decisions are delayed because reliable information is unavailable.

## Customer experience and growth

19. The website is disconnected from the sales or operating process behind it.
20. Current systems would struggle if customer or work volume doubled.

## Score interpretation

### 0–8 — Contained friction

The operating model is generally stable. Focus on one or two recurring problems before adding new technology.

### 9–18 — Growing coordination cost

Manual handoffs and fragmented context are creating avoidable work. A focused workflow review or integration may create meaningful leverage.

### 19–30 — System fragmentation

Several areas depend on people carrying information between tools. Map one end-to-end workflow and define the source of truth before buying more software.

### 31–40 — Operating model at risk

Fragmentation is likely affecting customer experience, visibility, speed and financial control. A structured Systems Review should become a near-term priority.
