# Trexiti Capability Statement — One-Page Copy

## TREXITI

### Digital systems for ambitious businesses.

Trexiti designs and builds the digital systems businesses use to sell, operate and grow.

We combine business analysis, design and engineering to create websites, software, operational systems and automation around how a company actually works.

---

## What we build

### Digital Experiences

Premium websites and customer journeys that communicate the value of the business, strengthen trust and connect attention to action.

### Custom Software

Purpose-built applications, customer portals, staff portals, booking systems, marketplaces, platforms and dashboards.

### Business Systems

CRM, sales, job and work-order management, scheduling, inventory, staff, finance workflows, documents and management reporting.

### Automation & Integration

Reliable workflows between websites, CRM, email, WhatsApp, calendars, payments, accounting software, cloud storage and external APIs.

---

## When Trexiti makes sense

- Customer information lives across WhatsApp, email, spreadsheets, paper or disconnected software.
- Staff repeatedly enter or reconcile the same information.
- Work, ownership, status, invoices or payments are difficult to see.
- The website no longer reflects the quality of the business.
- Customers have no clear digital way to book, pay, submit documents or track progress.
- Generic tools no longer fit the operating workflow.
- One focused digital improvement could remove a meaningful business constraint.

---

## How we work

**Discover** — understand the company, customers, workflows and objective.  
**Map** — make processes, handoffs, data and bottlenecks visible.  
**Design** — define the experience, architecture, roles and system boundary.  
**Build** — engineer the product using reliable modern technology.  
**Integrate** — connect useful tools and automate deliberate workflows.  
**Improve** — refine the system using real operational feedback.

---

## Engagement shapes

### Focused Build

One contained, high-leverage improvement for a smaller business or a clearly defined need.

### Connected Experience

A website or customer journey connected to CRM, booking, payments, communication or internal workflows.

### Custom System

A purpose-built application or operating platform around a specific business model.

### Systems Partnership

Ongoing analysis, development, integration and improvement across the business.

The right engagement is shaped by the problem—not the size or prestige of the company.
