# Trexiti Systems Review

## Primary title

### Make the way your business works visible.

## Supporting copy

A Trexiti Systems Review examines one workflow or a wider operating area to identify where work, information, ownership and decisions become fragmented.

The answer is not automatically custom software.

The review determines what should be simplified, clarified, integrated, automated or built.

## Suitable review subjects

- Customer inquiry to sale
- Booking to service delivery
- Quote approval to completed work
- Job scheduling and staff assignment
- Inventory request to replenishment
- Completed work to invoice and payment
- Customer document and approval workflows
- Management reporting
- Website-to-CRM handoffs
- One persistent small-business bottleneck

## What Trexiti examines

### People

Who performs the work, owns decisions and handles exceptions?

### Process

What sequence of actions, approvals and handoffs actually occurs?

### Customers

What does the customer experience, repeat, wait for or fail to see?

### Data

What information enters, changes, duplicates, disappears or becomes unreliable?

### Technology

Which tools are useful, disconnected, misused, redundant or missing?

## Review method

1. Define the business objective.
2. Select the workflow boundary.
3. Interview the people involved.
4. Map the current state.
5. Identify friction, risk and duplicate work.
6. Define ownership and source-of-truth rules.
7. Evaluate keep / integrate / replace / build decisions.
8. Design a practical future-state model.
9. Recommend the smallest valuable implementation path.

## Possible outputs

Depending on scope, the review may produce:

- current-state workflow map;
- problem and bottleneck summary;
- future-state system map;
- requirements and user-role outline;
- integration opportunities;
- automation opportunities;
- build-versus-buy recommendations;
- implementation phases;
- preliminary solution architecture;
- a focused proposal for the next step.

## Engagement paths after review

### No build required

Trexiti may recommend a process change or better use of an existing platform.

### Focused Build

One contained improvement, integration, workflow or digital experience.

### Connected System

Several functions connected around one operating model.

### Custom Platform

Purpose-built software where the business genuinely needs to own the system boundary.
